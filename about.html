Rodos designs, manufactures and installs stainless steel kitchens for villas and apartments — with a team of designers, engineers and installers under one roof.

Our story

Our journey began with a singular vision — to bring the enduring strength and beauty of stainless steel kitchens into every home.

Founded in the UAE, we have grown with the region's appetite for sophisticated interiors and durable materials. Backed by a talented team of designers, engineers and installation specialists, we have worked on hundreds of residential projects — from luxury villas to upscale apartments.

Our partnership with Silver Metals Industries lets us manufacture locally while keeping to international quality standards. That means one team is responsible for your kitchen from the first sketch to the last fitting.
